Figure~\ref{fig:colbert-vs-gpumvr} compares the recall--throughput trade-off of ColBERT and GPU-MVR on the three datasets. GPU-MVR consistently achieves higher throughput than ColBERT in the high-recall regime. At $\mathrm{Recall}@100 \geq 0.9$, GPU-MVR is 9.8$\times$, 7.5$\times$, and 4.7$\times$ faster on LoTTE, HotpotQA, and MS MARCO, respectively. At $\mathrm{Recall}@100 \geq 0.95$, the advantage remains substantial on LoTTE and MS MARCO, where GPU-MVR is 11.3$\times$ and 6.9$\times$ faster, respectively. ColBERT reaches a higher maximum recall in some cases; however, this gap may partly reflect conservative parameter choices for GPU-MVR and will be revisited in subsequent tuning. Overall, these results indicate that GPU-MVR provides a more favorable efficiency--effectiveness trade-off than ColBERT on all three datasets.
